London Airport Drop Off Charges

Travelling through London airports in 2026 means understanding the latest airport drop-off charges before you arrive. Most major airports across London now operate paid drop-off systems designed to reduce traffic congestion around terminals and encourage greater use of public transport.
While the charges and time limits vary from airport to airport, drivers who fail to follow the rules or miss payment deadlines may face expensive penalty notices. Below is a complete overview of the drop-off systems at all six major London airports.

Heathrow Airport Drop Off Charges

Heathrow Airport applies a Heathrow drop-off charge across all terminals, including Terminal 2, 3, 4, and 5. Drivers using the designated terminal forecourts are currently charged £5 per visit, regardless of how long they stop within the permitted time.

The airport uses automatic number plate recognition (ANPR) technology, and payments must be completed online or by phone before the deadline. Heathrow also offers free alternatives through its Long Stay car parks with shuttle bus services.

Gatwick Airport Drop Off Charges

Gatwick Airport operates paid drop-off zones outside both the North and South Terminals. Drivers are charged Gatwick drop off charge £10 for up to 10 minutes, with additional fees applied for longer stays.

Travellers who want to avoid the terminal charge can use the Long Stay car park, which offers free parking for a limited time along with free shuttle transfers to the terminal.

Stansted Airport Drop Off Charges

At Stansted Airport, the Express Set Down area closest to the terminal is a paid zone designed for quick passenger drop-offs. Stansted drop-off charges currently begin from £7 for short stays, with higher fees for extended visits.

Drivers looking for a free option can use the Mid Stay car park, which provides complimentary parking for a limited period along with a shuttle service to the terminal.

Luton Airport Drop Off Charges

Luton Airport offers both paid and free drop-off options. The Priority Luton Airport Drop-Off zone near the terminal currently costs £7 for up to 10 minutes, with additional per-minute charges after that period.

Passengers wanting to avoid the fee can use the Mid Stay parking area, which allows free short-term drop-offs and includes a shuttle transfer service.

Southend Airport Drop Off Charges

Southend Airport also operates designated Southend airport drop-off charges to areas close to the terminal entrance. Charges vary depending on the parking zone and duration of stay, with short-stay fees applied to terminal forecourt access.

Alternative parking and nearby transport options are available for travellers who want to avoid airport drop-off charges during busy periods.

London City Airport Drop Off Charges

London City Airport introduced its paid London City Airport drop-off charge system in 2026, becoming the last major London airport to remove free forecourt access. Drivers are charged £8 for up to 5 minutes, with an additional £1 per minute for stays between 6 and 10 minutes.

Unlike several other London airports, London City currently does not offer a dedicated free drop-off parking area. Many passengers instead use the Docklands Light Railway (DLR) or pre-booked London Airport Taxi services to avoid the strict terminal time limits.
